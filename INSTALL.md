# Installing Hangar Log on your iPhone (Windows PC)

You only do Part 1 once. After that, each new version is just Part 2.

## Part 1 — One-time setup on the PC

1. **Install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website.** Sideloadly needs the
   versions downloaded from Apple, not the Microsoft Store versions. If the
   Microsoft Store versions are already installed, uninstall them first.
   Sideloadly's home page (https://sideloadly.io) has direct links to the
   correct downloads.
2. **Install Sideloadly** from https://sideloadly.io (Windows download).
3. Restart the PC.

## Part 2 — Get the app file

1. Open https://github.com/dfwilley/Hangar-Voice-Log/releases
2. Under the newest build, click **HangarVoiceLog.ipa** to download it.

## Part 3 — Put it on the iPhone

1. Plug the iPhone into the PC with a USB cable. Unlock the phone. If it asks
   **Trust This Computer?**, tap **Trust** and enter your passcode.
2. Open Sideloadly. Your iPhone should appear in the **iDevice** box.
3. Type your Apple ID email in the **Apple account** box.
4. Drag **HangarVoiceLog.ipa** onto the big IPA icon in Sideloadly.
5. Click **Start**. Enter your Apple ID password when asked. (Sideloadly sends
   it to Apple only. If you use two-factor sign-in, enter the code that pops
   up on your phone.)
6. Wait for **Done.** at the bottom of Sideloadly's log.

## Part 4 — First launch on the iPhone (one time)

1. **Turn on Developer Mode:** Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll to the
   bottom → **Developer Mode** → On → **Restart**. After the restart, unlock
   the phone and tap **Turn On** when asked.
2. **Trust your Apple ID for apps:** Settings → General → VPN & Device
   Management → tap your Apple ID email → **Trust**.
3. Open **Hangar Log**. Allow the microphone and speech recognition when it
   asks.

## Every 7 days

Apps installed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. Either:

- **Automatic:** in Sideloadly, turn on **automatic refresh** for Hangar Log.
  The PC needs to be on and the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi (or plugged in).
- **Manual:** repeat Part 3 with the same file. Your entries, recordings and
  photos are kept as long as you use the same Apple ID.

## Updating to a new version

Download the newer file (Part 2) and repeat Part 3. Your entries are kept.

## Good to know

- Everything you record stays on the phone. Use **Share** or **Export All as
  Spreadsheet** to get entries off the phone.
- Your entries are also visible in the iPhone **Files** app under
  On My iPhone → Hangar Log.
- A free Apple ID can have only a few sideloaded apps at once (about three).
